JobPilot v0.1.17 — Scan status bar and on-demand report
Generated: 2026-08-11

Implemented
===========
1. The long scan-status pill keeps a fixed 590x46 CSS-pixel desktop footprint while its contents change during scanning.
2. During a scan the status pill shows real progress: completed/total, current source, concurrent-source count, and an in-place progress fill.
3. The separate "סרוק עכשיו" action button no longer morphs into a progress meter; it keeps its label and size and is only disabled while a scan is already running.
4. Scan completion no longer auto-opens the scan report and no completion report toast is shown. The persistent status pill updates silently.
5. Clicking the scan-status pill after completion opens the full report on demand.
6. The report was redesigned with six summary metrics, a dedicated errors section, and collapsed per-source details.
7. Light/dark mode and responsive layouts were added for the new scan status/report UI.
