JobPilot v0.1.16 — Scan performance, incremental results and freshness scoring
Generated: 2026-08-11

Implemented
===========
1. Freshness scoring no longer leaves live jobs at a misleading 50/100 when the ATS has no publish date.
   - Explicit recent publish dates: graded freshness up to 100.
   - Missing publish date but recently revalidated on the live source: 90 freshness and a small +2 ranking bonus.
   - Explicit old publish dates are still penalized.
   - Existing saved jobs are rescored on startup.
2. Source collection is now bounded and concurrent.
   - Up to 4 sources are collected in parallel.
   - Each source has a 45-second wall-clock timeout.
   - One stalled source can no longer block the entire scan for hours.
3. Results are committed source-by-source.
   - As soon as a source finishes, its jobs are saved/scored immediately.
   - Auto-queue is applied immediately for that source when enabled.
   - Frontend polling refreshes the currently visible Jobs/Dashboard/Sources/Applications screen after each completed source.
4. Scan progress now supports concurrent work.
   - Shows completed/total.
   - Shows the current source plus how many additional sources are scanning in parallel.
   - Shows the last completed source and number of new jobs from it.
5. Stale cleanup no longer deletes still-active jobs merely because they are older than two days.
   - Cleanup now applies only to jobs already marked inactive by their source.
   - This prevents valid jobs from disappearing before their source is rescanned.
